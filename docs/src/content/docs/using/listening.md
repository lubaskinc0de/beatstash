---
title: Listen to your library
description: Play your music through Navidrome or a Subsonic-compatible app.
---

## In a browser

Open the [Navidrome](https://www.navidrome.org/) address supplied by your administrator and sign in with your linked account. The bot shows the configured address in **Help > How to listen**.

Your personal library, the shared library, and any existing libraries you can access are available through Navidrome. Imported likes become stars, and imported playlists appear once their tracks are indexed.

## In an app

Choose a client from [Navidrome's apps list](https://www.navidrome.org/apps/). Add a Navidrome or Subsonic server with:

- The same HTTPS address you use in the browser.
- Your Navidrome login.
- Your Navidrome password.

Offline downloads, background playback, and device integration depend on the client. Check its own documentation before choosing it. You do not need a streaming subscription to play files already stored on your server.

## Through Telegram

Use **Send the file** on a track's card to receive its audio in Telegram. Albums can be sent as public listening links when the administrator has enabled that feature. See [sharing in Telegram](./sharing.md).

If a new track is visible in the bot but missing from the player, allow Navidrome to scan it. If an imported playlist is incomplete, some tracks may still be downloading or waiting for indexing.

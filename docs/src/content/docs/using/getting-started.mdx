Someone runs a music server and invited you. You'll get a library of your own on it, fill it by sending files to a Telegram bot, and listen in a browser or a phone app. You don't install anything on a server.

## Accept the invitation

Open the invite link and press **Start** in Telegram. An invite works for one person and expires after seven days by default. If yours expired or was already used, ask the administrator for a new one.

When the bot asks, choose a [Navidrome](https://www.navidrome.org/) login: 3 to 32 characters, using Latin letters, digits, dots, hyphens, or underscores. You'll sign in to players with it. The bot creates the account and shows the password once.

<Screenshot name="register-login" alt="The bot asks a newly invited person to choose a Navidrome login." />

:::caution
Save the password before you delete the message. The bot doesn't show it again.
:::

If you already have an account on this Navidrome, choose **I already have an account** and send `login password`. The bot deletes that message right away. An account linked to another participant can't be linked again.

Once you're in, the bot opens its home screen. Everything starts from here:

<Screenshot name="home" alt="The home screen of the bot with the Music, Import from a service, Help, and Settings buttons." />

## Link an account later

Open **Settings > Navidrome account > Link**. To switch to a different account, use **Link another**.

A linked regular account sees your personal library, the shared library, and any [existing libraries](../reference/glossary.md#existing-library) the administrator gave it. It loses access to other participants' personal libraries. A Navidrome administrator account keeps seeing every library.

## Add your first track

Send the bot an audio file in your private chat with it, or forward one from another chat. Wait for 👍, then open [Navidrome or your player](./listening.mdx). Navidrome may need a moment to scan the file.

The track is in **Music > Mine**. From its card you can get the file or put the track in the shared library.

## Change the language

Open **Settings > Language**. English and Russian are built in; the administrator can add more.

If you open the bot without an invite, it says the server is invite-only. Ask the server's administrator for an invite.

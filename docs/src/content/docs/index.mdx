## What gets in the way

A self-hosted library is pleasant to listen to and tedious to fill. A friend sends you a track in Telegram, and to hear it in [Navidrome](https://www.navidrome.org/) you save the file, copy it to the server over SSH, and wait for a scan. A friend wants their own music on your server, so you create an account, decide which libraries they see, and upload their files for them. You want to leave a streaming service, and nothing brings your likes and playlists along.

beatstash takes these chores into a Telegram chat. Navidrome keeps doing the playback.

<Feature title="Add music from Telegram" screenshot="upload-reactions" alt="Tracks forwarded to the bot, each marked with a thumbs-up once it is in the library.">
Forward a track or send files from your phone. The bot fills in the tags, skips tracks you already have, and files the music into artist and album folders. A 👍 means the track is in your library.

[How uploading works](./using/uploading.mdx)
</Feature>

<Feature title="Share music in any chat" screenshot="inline-sent" alt="A track sent into a chat with np, captioned with its album, year, and genre." flip>
Type `@your_music_bot np` in any conversation to send what you're playing, or search your library by artist or title. Friends get the audio with its album and year, not just a song name to look up.

[Sharing in Telegram](./using/sharing.mdx)
</Feature>

<Feature title="One server for your friends" screenshot="shared-tab" alt="A track in the shared library showing who shared it, with Already yours and Send the file buttons.">
Send an invite link. Each person gets a Navidrome account, a personal library, and a storage limit you choose. When someone finds a track worth passing around, they share it, and others take it into their own library.

[Libraries and quotas](./using/libraries.md)
</Feature>

<Feature title="Leave streaming with your collection" screenshot="zvuk-plan" alt="The plan of a Zvuk import with the number of tracks, their size, and the Start the import button." flip>
Connect a supported service and import your likes, saved albums, and playlists. After the first import, the bot keeps pulling in what you save there.

[Supported sources](./import/sources.md)
</Feature>
